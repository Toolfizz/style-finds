5 Romantic Pre-Wedding Couple Poses 💕

Save these simple posing ideas for your pre-wedding shoot. Each one is designed to look natural, romantic and easy to recreate.

Pose 1

Close Embrace

Stand close, keep the bodies angled slightly, and let one partner rest a hand on the other's chest.

Pose 2

Walking Hand in Hand

Walk slowly and look at each other instead of the camera for a natural candid feel.

Pose 3

Forehead Touch

Bring the foreheads together softly and relax the shoulders for an intimate portrait.

Pose 4

The Twirl

Let the groom raise one hand and guide a gentle spin so the dress creates movement.

Pose 5

Seated Romance

Sit close, lean toward each other and keep the hands relaxed for a soft editorial pose.
